## 3. Candidate ideas

### I1. Morning pages
- Mechanism: daily freewriting before other tasks
- Mechanism category: habit
- Why it applies: creative block clears with low-stakes daily practice
- Support basis: assumption-backed: mornings are freer than evenings
- Decision-criteria fit: highest weekly word potential at lowest cost
- Expected impact: high
- Assumptions and dependencies: alarm and quiet space available
- Effort: low
- Risk: low
- Confidence: moderate
- What would disconfirm it: fewer than 500 words after one week
- Cheapest decisive experiment: write morning pages for 7 days; metric: words/week; pass/fail: >=2000; duration: 7d; cost/effort: low

### I2. Workshop
- Mechanism: enroll in a short external writing class
- Mechanism category: class
- Why it applies: structured deadlines and feedback may unlock a draft
- Support basis: hypothesis: workshop unlocks draft
- Decision-criteria fit: stronger learning support, higher cost
- Expected impact: high
- Assumptions and dependencies: workshop seat and tuition budget
- Effort: medium
- Risk: medium
- Confidence: low
- What would disconfirm it: no draft progress after first two sessions
- Cheapest decisive experiment: attend first two sessions and write 1000 words; metric: draft words; pass/fail: >=1000; duration: 14d; cost/effort: medium

### I3. Accountability
- Mechanism: weekly partner check-ins on word targets
- Mechanism category: social
- Why it applies: social commitment increases follow-through
- Support basis: hypothesis: accountability increases output
- Decision-criteria fit: improves consistency with modest schedule cost
- Expected impact: medium
- Assumptions and dependencies: willing writing partner
- Effort: low
- Risk: low
- Confidence: low
- What would disconfirm it: missed check-ins or no word gain after two weeks
- Cheapest decisive experiment: two weekly check-ins with a partner; metric: sessions kept; pass/fail: 2 of 2; duration: 14d; cost/effort: low
